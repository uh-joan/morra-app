      Notes

      Scheduler: Worker-driven lookahead (never main-thread setInterval for audio). Hand onset = fingertip-velocity spike then settle (stabilization instant, not the spike peak). Voice onset = energy-threshold VAD with adaptive noise floor, timestamped at the AudioWorklet render-quantum boundary. Both channels are converted to the performance.now() timeline via the AudioContext clock mapping before comparison to the audible beat time (scheduled time + outputLatency).
